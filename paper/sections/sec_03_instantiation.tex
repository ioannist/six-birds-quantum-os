\section{Instantiation on quantum error correction}
\label{sec:instantiation}

\subsection{The domain objects}
\label{sec:domain}

\paragraph{Codes.} The prototype uses two code families in four instances, all declared and frozen before implementation: REP(3), REP(5) and SURF(3) as the primary instances, and SURF(5) only for scaling spot-checks. % F-163
REP($d$) is the classical repetition code with checks $Z_iZ_{i+1}$. It protects against bit flips only; read as a quantum stabilizer code, its phase-flip distance is 1. Experiments on REP codes therefore track only the protected logical observable. They use bit-flip noise, except the hidden-state studies on REP(3), whose depolarizing noise the code sees only through its bit-flip component. SURF($d$) is the rotated surface code~\cite{kitaev2003anyons,bravyi1998boundary,dennis2002topological}; SURF(3) has 9 data qubits and 8 checks. % F-163
Code conventions follow the standard rotated layout~\cite{fowler2012surfacecodes,tomita2014lowdistance}, and the declared distances of SURF(3) and SURF(5) are verified in the test suite by exhaustive search for low-weight undetected logical errors.

\paragraph{Noise.} Five noise models are used, all stochastic Pauli noise at the code-capacity level unless stated otherwise.
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item N1: independent bit flips with probability $p$.
\item N2: independent depolarizing noise with probability $p$.
\item N3: N2 plus an \emph{unmodeled} correlated $ZZ$ error on one declared qubit pair with probability $q$. This is the drift model: the decoder's declared model still believes N2.
\item N4: a hidden two-state Markov mode that switches the depolarizing rate between $p_0$ and $3p_0$. The mode is not observable.
\item N5: a latching proxy for leakage. One designated qubit irreversibly becomes fully depolarizing, at rate $r$ per round.
\end{itemize}
N4 is a telegraph-type rate fluctuation of the kind that two-level-system defects produce in superconducting qubits~\cite{klimov2018fluctuations}, and temporally correlated noise of this kind is known to degrade surface-code memories~\cite{kam2025nonmarkovian}. N5 is deliberately crude; real leakage in stabilizer circuits is more structured~\cite{ghosh2013leakage,mcewen2021removing}. Sections~\ref{sec:e8}--\ref{sec:e9} also use circuit-level noise: Stim-generated rotated-surface-code memory circuits~\cite{gidney2021stim} with depolarizing and measurement-flip errors at every location, decoded by minimum-weight perfect matching~\cite{dennis2002topological,edmonds1965blossom} with PyMatching~\cite{higgott2022pymatching,higgott2025sparseblossom}.

\paragraph{The moment engine.} Everything exact rests on one identity. A Pauli error and a check or logical observable are both vectors in $\mathbb{F}_2^{2n}$~\cite{gottesman1997stabilizer}. The detection bit is their symplectic pairing $\langle a, e\rangle$, and the corresponding $\pm1$ observable is $\sigma_a(e) = (-1)^{\langle a,e\rangle}$. Because $\sigma_a \sigma_b = \sigma_{a \oplus b}$, every covariance block in Eq.~\eqref{eq:xi} reduces to means of such observables. Under independent qubit noise, each mean $\mathbb{E}[\sigma_a]$ is a product of per-qubit rational factors. N3's correlated injection, being independent of the rest, multiplies this product by its own factor, and hidden-mode models are evaluated one mode at a time. Every covariance block is therefore exactly computable without sampling. The same identity later makes the diagnosis of Section~\ref{sec:diagnosis} possible. Means of XOR-combinations of checks \emph{are} the Walsh--Hadamard coefficients of the joint syndrome distribution~\cite{odonnell2014analysis}, so the whole $2^{8}$-outcome syndrome distribution of SURF(3) can be recovered exactly from the moment engine. % F-163
The same Boolean-Fourier fact underlies identifiability results for learning Pauli channels from syndrome statistics~\cite{wagner2022paulichannels}.

\subsection{Scope}
\label{sec:fences}

The following limits were fixed in the design documents before implementation. They bind every result in this paper. % C-04, C-05

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{No quantum hardware.} All evidence is classical simulation of stochastic Pauli and classical Markov models, plus Stim circuit-level sampling.
\item \emph{The residual is relative to an estimator class.} The prototype's $\Xi$ is the residual covariance of the best affine predictor of the logical $\pm1$ observables from the scheduled-check $\pm1$ observables, optionally augmented with degree-2 products. $\Xi = 0$ certifies exact affine coverage over the declared features. A nonzero $\Xi$ certifies, and prices, a coverage gap for that class. It does \emph{not} assert that no nonlinear decoder covers the gap; the degree ladder shows how the residual contracts as the feature degree grows.
\item \emph{No Born-rule, Bell or coherent-dynamics claims.} Stochastic Pauli noise is a standard, declared restriction. It leaves out coherent errors~\cite{bravyi2018coherent} and quantum-bath non-Markovian dynamics~\cite{terhal2005nonmarkovian}. The temporal correlations the prototype does model (N4, N5) are classical hidden-state correlations layered on stochastic Pauli noise. SBT's own paper on quantum theory works at exactly this packaging level~\cite{sbt-qm}.
\item \emph{No asymptotic threshold claims.} Every result concerns a finite carrier.
\item \emph{Certificates are computed, not fitted.} Residuals and quotients are computed exactly on declared finite models, as are the idempotence defect, retention error and multiplicity of the existence certificate for the REP(3) packages under bit-flip noise. The remaining existence-certificate quantities (closure deficits and route mismatches everywhere, and all components for SURF(3) and the hidden-state models) are computed numerically on the same declared models, without sampling or fitting. Fitted macro models are used only as diagnostics. Sampled outcomes, including the E5 payoff study of predictors trained on part of a trajectory, are reported as measurements, never as certificates.
\item \emph{The audits are independent.} Stability does not establish nontrivial storage: a trivialized decoder is perfectly stable, which is why multiplicity is a separate check. Coverage and existence do not imply one another, and neither do memory witnesses and drift. Each audit is certified or falsified on its own, and outcomes are never summed across audits.
\end{enumerate}
