\section{Deviations log (summary)}
\label{app:deviations}

The repository's deviations file records every divergence between the frozen designs and the running code. It has 11 dated entries, summarized here in a few sentences each; dates and full reasoning are in the file. No entry changed a frozen bar. % C-38, F-165

\paragraph{Build phase.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Finite-horizon route mismatch for N5} (E4). The latching model is absorbing, so the stationary-weight form of $RM$ degenerates. A declared finite-horizon variant was used, and the guard is documented. % F-099
\item \emph{Length of the predictive-gap stream} (E4). The fixed length of the proxy's stream can wash out fast-absorbing finite-horizon effects. This is noted as a caveat on $\Delta_{\mathrm{pred}}$, not silently retuned. % F-100
\item \emph{Packaged belief automaton} (E5). The minimal decoder machine is built on a declared, quantized packaged-belief automaton rather than on transport checks of linear kernels. No general impossibility of closed finite catalogs is assumed (entry 10). % F-101
\item \emph{Bounded-weight cross-check estimator} (E1). The cross-check originally used an uncentered estimator instead of the affine $A^{*}$ that the decoder actually is. It was corrected before any follow-up work began, giving 4/4 on REP(3) and 14/16 on REP(5), with two genuine failures of the affine class. This entry was added after an external review found the fix documented in prose but missing from the log. % F-101b
\item \emph{Payoff study} (E5). A post-registration study of next-round log-likelihood, labeled \gMeasured, which alters no frozen verdict. An addendum records that its run-length machines read one syndrome bit and the logical increment rather than syndrome history alone. % F-102
\item \emph{Granularity of E7's null calibration.} The own-null thresholds of W2a and W2b were calibrated once per sample size $N$ rather than once per pair of $N$ and seed. This was declared; the baseline's calibration was unaffected. % F-103
\item \emph{E8 scope and pilot-then-freeze.} Distance 3 only, one global drift scenario, the full pair family, no sequential rung. The pilot seed range and the resulting frozen bars are logged verbatim. % F-104
\item \emph{E9 corrections, before and after the first freeze.} The drift scenario was changed from a uniform rescaling to a single elevated channel, the timeline was divided into epochs, and the witness policy needed two pilot-driven fixes: a design that could permanently blind itself after a false alarm, then a noisy-reference bug in the self-correcting version. After the first frozen run, the budget-matched schedule was corrected to exact per-seed event matching, and the experiment was re-run and re-frozen. % F-105
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Audit of the mathematics and executable certificates.}
\begin{enumerate}[resume]
\item \emph{Exact arithmetic and closure domains.} Exhaustive pricing enumeration, greedy selection, the chain-rule discrepancy, E1's saturation check and E3's MMSE comparison are carried out in rational arithmetic throughout, and uniform stationarity is checked exactly. Closure computations handle arbitrary label sets and skip zero-weight cells, and package construction rejects invalid probability data. Frozen orders and verdicts are unaffected. % F-178
\item \emph{E5 sensor, scoring and quotient premises.} The belief update reads the syndrome actually supplied to it: the logical bit is marginalized out, successive cumulative syndromes are differenced, and the known initial state is not treated as an observation. The payoff study fits its rounding grid on the training half and scores every predictor on the held-out half. Hidden-model future catalogs include identity continuations with current tests, so that predictive classes refine current classes (N5 has 8 predictive classes; its witnesses, MaxFiber and $\Delta^{\max}$ are as reported). The machine is treated as a quantized predictor and minimized exactly for its next-syndrome predictions. % F-030, F-102, F-171, F-172
\item \emph{E9's uniform-drift rationale.} The single-channel drift rests on the pilot's observation of only a very small decoder difference under uniform drift, not on an invariance argument: uniform rescaling changes the matching weights, and not in proportion. The scenario and the frozen bars are unaffected. % F-176
\end{enumerate}
